Founder edition: 413 of 1,000 minted (41.3%). Static snapshot — with JavaScript, figures refresh live from the contract.

How to read this wall: the Founder edition is capped at the first 1,000 passports. When "Founder slots remaining" reaches zero, minting continues as the Standard edition — the passport, the check-ins and the levels work exactly the same. Check-ins are counted once per passport per UTC day, so the check-in total only grows through real daily activity.

Want to verify a single passport? Call attributesOf(tokenId) on the AgentPass contract, 0x2CaB70C426F92C0d08aEb7cFe41b27b746feEb09 — it returns owner, edition, current streak, best streak, check-ins and level in one read. Full specification: /agents. Or check any address — passport, level and AgentScore — through the free verification API.
